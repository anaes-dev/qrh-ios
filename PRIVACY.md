# Privacy policy

**QRH for iOS** · effective 23 October 2026

**Developer:** Matthew Round. Contact: [qrh@matthewround.com](mailto:qrh@matthewround.com) or
[GitHub](https://github.com/anaes-dev/qrh-ios/issues).

**Scope:** this policy covers only the app I build and publish on GitHub and the App Store. The source code is
shared under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), so others may
modify and distribute their own versions; those may handle data differently and are not covered
here.

**What I collect:** nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics or adverts, and its settings stay
on your device until you delete the app. It adds guidelines to Spotlight search and Home Screen
quick actions on your device only.

**Websites:** the guidelines themselves are built into the app. Only if you ask for the original
PDF of a guideline is it downloaded from the Association of Anaesthetists' website, and web links
you tap open in your browser; those sites handle your request under their own privacy policies,
and nothing about it is sent to me.

**App Store:** Apple collects install statistics, and crash reports if you have agreed to share
them with app developers, under [its own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
I see only anonymous totals in App Store Connect, use them only to improve the app, and keep no
copies.

**Children:** the app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

**Your rights:** as I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct
or delete. You can still contact me with any question, or complain to the
[ICO](https://ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

**Changes** are published here, with past versions kept in this repository's history.
